# Third-party components

Retro Portal can integrate with third-party software that keeps its own license and copyright terms.

- EmulatorJS — browser emulation frontend/runtime; installed separately by `scripts/install-emulatorjs.sh`.
- RetroArch/libretro cores distributed by EmulatorJS retain their respective licenses.
- Flycast WASM integration is experimental and not bundled by default.
- Nginx, Docker, Certbot and certbot-dns-cloudflare are installed from their own upstream/package sources.
- The optional demo/homebrew installer uses redistributable projects only.

No commercial ROMs, BIOS images or official game artwork are distributed by this repository.
